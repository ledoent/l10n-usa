This module determines, aggregates and exports US **1099-NEC** and
**1099-MISC** information returns from vendor bills.

Its core is a **reportability determination engine**: whether a payment is
reportable is the intersection of

* the payee's **W-9 tax classification** (corporations are generally exempt;
  an LLC is reportable or exempt depending on its C/S/P sub-class),
* the **payment category** (driven by the expense account's 1099 box), with
  corporate-exemption overrides for attorney and medical payments,
* the **payment method** (bills paid by card / third-party network are
  excluded, as the processor reports them on 1099-K), and
* the **effective-dated threshold** for the box and year.

It produces per-payee, per-box report lines and exports a CSV suitable for
upload to the IRS **IRIS** Taxpayer Portal. Transmission is pluggable: a base
adapter is provided so a 3rd-party filer API (Tax1099, Avalara/Track1099) or
direct IRIS A2A can be added later.

Configuration
=============

#. Map expense accounts to 1099 boxes: *Accounting > Configuration >
   Accounting > Chart of Accounts*, open an account and set its **1099 Box**
   (e.g. a "Contractor services" account → 1099-NEC Box 1, a "Legal fees"
   account → 1099-NEC Box 1 with **Reportable to Corporations** ticked).
#. On each vendor, set the **W-9 Tax Classification** (and, for an LLC, the
   C/S/P sub-class), TIN type and W-9 status under the *US 1099 / W-9* tab.
#. Review the seeded **1099 Boxes** and their effective-dated thresholds under
   *Accounting > Configuration > 1099 Boxes* (the $600 → $2,000 change for
   2026 is pre-loaded; re-verify against the final IRS instructions each year).

Usage
=====

#. Tick **Exclude from 1099** on any vendor bill paid by credit card or a
   third-party network (it will be reported by the processor on 1099-K).
#. Go to *Accounting > Reporting > 1099 > Generate 1099 Report*, choose the
   calendar year and generate.
#. Review the report lines (reportable rows are highlighted; below-threshold
   rows are muted). Select the lines and use *Action > Export 1099 CSV* to
   download a CSV for the IRS IRIS Taxpayer Portal.

The figures aggregate posted vendor bills/refunds on an accrual (bill-date)
basis. Cash-basis aggregation by payment date is a planned enhancement.
